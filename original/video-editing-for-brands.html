Planning your project

Questions about video editing for brands and creators.

Can you edit footage I already have?

Yes. Existing footage can be the starting point. Share what you have, the message you want to communicate, where the video will be used, and a reference for the style or pace you have in mind.

What types of videos can we discuss?

Short-form edits, promotional content, launch videos, and creator assets are all relevant starting points. The final scope depends on the available footage, graphics, intended length, and required formats.

What should I send for a video editing quote?

Describe the amount of source footage, intended video length, number of edits, and deadline. Mention captions, motion graphics, music, and platform versions you may need so those requirements can be discussed before work starts.

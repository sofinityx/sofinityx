Planning your project

Questions about ad creative and campaign design.

What can a campaign design project include?

The scope can cover creative concepts, message angles, ad visuals, and launch assets adapted to agreed formats. Start with the offer, audience, and channels so we can identify the materials the campaign needs.

Can the creative match my existing brand and landing page?

Yes. Share your brand assets and the page people will visit after clicking. Keeping the offer, visual direction, and call to action consistent helps the campaign feel connected from the first impression to the next step.

What should I include in a campaign brief?

Include the offer, target audience, channels, required sizes or formats, available assets, and launch date. If you have previous creative or performance feedback, share that context as well.

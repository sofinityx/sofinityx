Planning your project

Questions about website and landing page design.

Do I need a landing page or a full website?

A landing page suits one focused offer, campaign, or launch with a clear next step. A full website is more useful when visitors need to explore several services, projects, or parts of your business. Your offer and the questions customers ask help determine the structure.

Can you help redesign an existing website?

Yes. Share your current website and what feels unclear or outdated. We can discuss the page structure, messaging, visual direction, and mobile experience, then agree which parts need to change.

What should I prepare before asking for a quote?

Send your current site if you have one, the pages or offer you need, examples of work you like, and your preferred timing. Mention any platform or development requirements so the design and implementation scope can be agreed together.

Planning your project

Questions about content projects.

Do I need a script or a brief?

No. Tell me what you want to make, who it’s for, and what you want people to do or feel afterwards. I’ll turn it into a short script and a plan for you to approve before anything is animated.

How long does a project take?

Usually three to seven days from an approved plan, depending on length and detail. Graphics are often quicker. Tight deadlines, like a fundraising day or an Eid campaign, can often be arranged if you mention them early.

Can it match our brand or our own characters?

Yes. Everything can use your colours, logo, fonts and tone, and characters can be designed around your audience. The style in the reel is one example, not a limit.

Do you work in Arabic?

Yes. Scripts, on-screen text and captions can be in English, Arabic or both, and we can talk through your project in either language.

About Sofi

Visual craft shaped into product clarity.

I began in visual and commercial design, developing a strong foundation in composition, hierarchy, branding, and communication. My work later expanded into digital products, where those skills became part of a broader process involving user flows, interaction systems, technical constraints, and product strategy.

Today, I combine product thinking with strong visual execution to design experiences that are functional, memorable, and carefully considered.

Planning your project

Questions about brand identity and visual direction.

Do I need a logo or a complete brand identity?

A logo is one part of an identity. If your website, social content, and other materials feel disconnected, a shared system of color, typography, marks, and layout direction can help them feel consistent. The right scope depends on where the brand will appear.

Can you work with my existing identity?

Yes. Share your current logo, brand materials, and the parts you want to keep. We can explore a focused refinement or a broader visual direction depending on what needs to change.

What helps you understand my brand?

Tell me what you offer, who you want to reach, and what makes your business different. Existing assets, visual references, and a list of intended applications help shape a practical identity brief.

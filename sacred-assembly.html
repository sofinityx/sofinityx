Website · Client Portal · Visual System

The Sacred Assembly

A website and members’ portal for an educational consultancy that helps founders build legal psychedelic churches and plant medicine ministries. The site earns trust with a reverent, hand-engraved look; the portal turns a complicated legal and spiritual process into one guided workspace.

Role
Web Design & Development, Portal UX/UI, Information Architecture, Visual System
Platform
Responsive website and secure client portal
Status
Live at thesacredassembly.love and still evolving
Scope
Marketing site, pricing and intake, 33-module portal, member tools, AI document drafting

Overview

Sacred in feel. Structured underneath.

The Sacred Assembly supports healers, ceremony facilitators, church founders and community leaders who want to create sacred healing spaces the right way. Forming a psychedelic church touches entity formation, religious-freedom law, sacrament doctrine, safety protocols, branding and community building, and most founders try to piece it together from scattered sources.

The work had two halves: a public website that feels as reverent as the communities it serves while staying clear about what is offered, and a client portal that walks founders through every phase, step by step, with the tools to do the work in one place.

The Challenge

A sensitive subject

Visitors arrive cautious. The design had to feel spiritual and trustworthy, never like a trend site or a loophole.

Education, not legal advice

Every page had to be clear that this is educational consulting and that legal matters belong with a qualified attorney.

A huge amount of content

Legal foundations, doctrine, safety, branding, research and operations had to stay findable instead of becoming a wall of text.

Founders at different stages

Some are starting from scratch, others already run a community. Both needed a clear next step.

Different sacraments, different paths

Psilocybin, ayahuasca, peyote, iboga and others each come with their own traditions and legal context.

The Website

A calm path from first visit to first conversation.

The homepage opens with a single promise and two clear actions, then answers the visitor’s questions in order: what this is, who it is for, what is inside, which sacrament it fits, and what people usually ask.

Visual System

Gold engraving on deep forest green.

The look borrows from old botanical prints and sacred manuscripts: fine gold linework, vine borders, parchment for long reading, and an elegant serif. It feels reverent and established, which matters for a subject where trust comes first.

Engraved, not psychedelic

Instead of the neon swirls people expect, the brand uses quiet gold engraving. It signals care and seriousness to founders, partners and attorneys alike.

Parchment for reading

Long passages such as the mission statement sit on warm parchment cards, so they read like a document rather than marketing copy.

An illustration for every sacrament

Each medicine gets its own engraved botanical, so the “choose your sacrament” step feels personal and respectful of its tradition.

Ornament with restraint

Vine borders and dividers frame the page, while the content area stays clean, readable and fast to scan.

The Client Portal

Thirty-three modules, in the order founders need them.

The portal is the product. It is organized into eight phases, from orientation and legal formation to doctrine and safety, community and brand, education, cultivation and operations. Practical tools sit inside the flow instead of in a separate app.

Phases, not a pile of pages

Navigation follows the real order of the work, with a start checklist and progress timeline so founders always know what comes next.

Tools where the work happens

An AI document drafter, template library, document vault, compliance dashboard, member registry, member email, incident log and donation tracker live next to the guidance they support.

Clear boundaries up front

Before entering, members read and accept an educational-use disclaimer, which reminds them to work with an attorney and accountant.

Built to grow with the law

A decriminalization map, research library and knowledge base can be updated as laws change, so the portal stays current.

Visitor Journey

Arrive on a clear promise Read the mission See who it is for Explore what is inside Choose a sacrament Compare the options Request personal pricing Fill in the founder intake Enter the portal

Pricing & Intake

Personal packages, one honest comparison.

Because every founder’s situation is different, pricing is tailored rather than fixed. The pricing page explains what is included, shows plainly what founders face without the portal, and leads to a step-by-step founder intake or a free consultation.

Outcome

  • A live website that presents a sensitive subject with warmth, clarity and credibility.
  • A single portal that replaces scattered research with a guided, phase-by-phase path.
  • Practical tools for documents, compliance, members and donations inside the same workspace.
  • A visual system and content structure that can keep growing as laws and the community evolve.

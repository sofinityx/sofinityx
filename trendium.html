Trendium

A released Windows Electron desktop app for crypto trend awareness. Trendium fetches public market candle data, calculates indicators and scoring locally, and presents multi-timeframe trend pressure, market phase, timeframe agreement, and directional risk.

Overview

Crypto market context without buy or sell signals.

Trendium is for crypto traders and learners who want a clearer way to understand market structure without manually interpreting every timeframe and indicator. It turns candles, timeframe behavior, trend pressure, and risk context into structured dashboards, graphs, score states, and plain-English explanations.

The product is intentionally framed as educational market awareness, not financial advice, prediction, trade execution, or a signal system.

The Problem

Raw indicator overload

Trading platforms often overwhelm users with raw indicators, dense charts, and fragmented context.

Timeframe fragmentation

Users frequently switch between separate tools and timeframes to understand the broader market structure.

Beginner interpretation gap

Learners can struggle to understand indicators in context, especially during transitions, compression, and conflicting timeframes.

Responsible product language

The interface needed to stay informative while avoiding direct buy/sell signals, predictions, guaranteed outcomes, or profit framing.

Changing data

Large amounts of live and historical market data had to remain visually manageable and stable enough to review.

Key Design Decisions

Readable market-state labels

Multiple indicators are translated into beginner-safe labels such as market phase, pressure weakening, recovery attempt, compression, transition, and higher timeframe conflict.

Trend pressure score

A -100 to +100 pressure score gives users a quick visual sense of directional pressure while preserving more detailed graph and timeframe context.

Risk separate from direction

Directional risk bars separate market direction from risk awareness so trend pressure does not imply a trade recommendation.

Timeframes without crowding

Higher, middle, and lower timeframe grouping keeps the interface scannable while still supporting deeper review.

Educational tone

The app avoids words like buy, sell, signal, prediction, guaranteed, win rate, and profit. It uses market-awareness terminology instead.

Stable graph behavior

Closed historical graph points are cached during the app session to reduce visual shifting while live candles remain clearly marked as changeable.

Iteration

Refining hierarchy, trust, and release readiness.

Recent improvements included a more premium dashboard hero area, cleaner coin cards, a connected coin-detail header, easier dashboard return navigation, refined modal close buttons, UTC graph hover labels, closed/live candle states, coin images in detail headers, separated Store/direct licensing behavior, and production obfuscation before packaging.

Constraints

  • Windows Electron desktop app
  • Public Bybit OHLCV/kline candle data
  • Requires internet for live public market data
  • No private exchange API keys
  • No exchange account access
  • No trade execution
  • No direct buy or sell signals
  • Financial disclaimer requirements
  • Live candles can change until closed
  • Production obfuscation can add slight startup overhead

Outcome

  • Launched as a functional Windows desktop product and continues to be improved after release.
  • Established a reusable component and visual system.
  • Unified several market-analysis workflows inside one product.
  • Created a foundation for future features and product expansion.
